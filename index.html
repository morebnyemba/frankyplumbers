FAQ

Common questions

Do you charge a call-out fee?

We charge a small assessment fee, and we waive it completely if you go ahead with the repair. You'll always know the price before any work begins.

How fast can you get to me in an emergency?

For most Harare suburbs, we arrive within 60 minutes, 24/7. While you wait, shut off the main water valve and switch off your geyser at the distribution board.

Are your plumbers qualified and insured?

Yes. Every technician is a certified plumber, background-checked, and covered by our public liability insurance.

Do you guarantee your work?

All workmanship is guaranteed for 12 months. Parts carry the manufacturer's warranty. If something we fixed fails, we come back and sort it at no charge.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash (USD or ZiG), bank transfer, EcoCash and card. Commercial clients can apply for monthly accounts.
